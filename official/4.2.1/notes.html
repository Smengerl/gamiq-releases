A delivery after 4.2.0: the update screen, the settings of each game type, a manual with pictures, and a page that stays small as game types are added. Works with units of the same MAJOR version.
Highlights4
**An illustrated manual on the release site, one per release; the guide's chapter Hilfe becomes a link to it**. The manual lies in the folder of the release next to its image (official/<version>/anleitung.html, guide.html) and has pictures of what the displays show; the lock screen of the phone page links the manual of the version the unit runs, in a new window. A phone on the unit's own hotspot has no internet and cannot open it.
#450
The settings of each game type are kept on their owntime bar's total, colour cycle's interval and basketball's periods no longer overwrite each other. A unit that updates from 4.2.0 starts once with the standard settings of every game type: the old single slot is not read.
#438
The update screenthe percent in the digit font, its digits keep their place while it counts, no ball on the ring (the shot clock's ring too), a green check mark after a successful update and a red cross after a failure or a rollback.
#455#456#457#467#468
The page loads only the first game type's entry; the entry of every other game type is a module, loaded when the system plays itthe page is 15.5 KB instead of 16.2 KB, and a new game type costs it nothing. The size budgets are derived from the time the page may take (150 ms at every visit, 30 KB for the page, 12 KB for a module) and measured on the pages the build makes.

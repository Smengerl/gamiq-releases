Several games on one WiFi, for example two courts in a hall: each game has its own name, its own shot clocks and its own phones, and a phone can move a shot clock from one game to another. Works with units of the same MAJOR version; update every unit of v3.0.0 to v3.0.1 or later before a unit of this version joins the same WiFi.
What is new7
Gamesa unit belongs to one game. The game has a name (up to 20 bytes, the default is "Spiel" / "Game") that the page shows in its header, and a game of up to four shot clocks. Game names are unique on a WiFi.
#68
The Spiele popupnext to the name, the button lists this game with its shot clocks and the other games on the WiFi. Here a phone renames the game, moves a shot clock into another game, or lets it start a game of its own, which gets a name at once. A shot clock that moves is shown in its new game within seconds; if the move does not work, it stays where it was and the popup says why (game full, name taken, a game running).
Not during a gamewhile the clock runs or is paused no shot clock can be assigned to another game.
A Master alone may join another gameit restarts there as a Slave.
One popup at a timeopening the Einstellungen or the Spiele closes the other; a tap in the header or beside the popup closes it.
The guidehas a fourth chapter, Mehrere Spiele.
For people who buildthe unit link and the REST API carry the system (sys), /api/system lists the other games (systems), and a config can carry join; the contracts link.md and rest.md describe it.

Known limits3

#238
A game has at most four shot clocks; more than two units were tested on virtual units only, because the bench has two units.
The Master of a game with other shot clocks cannot move; a Master alone can.
